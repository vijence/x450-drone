# Pre-power checklist (bench)

- [ ] **Props OFF**
- [ ] Visual check: no loose strands, solder bridges or exposed copper
- [ ] Multimeter continuity: battery + to battery −, **no short**
- [ ] Multimeter: polarity correct at XT60 and at the PM02 output
- [ ] First power-up of a new or modified harness **through a smoke stopper**
- [ ] Battery voltage checked (BX100) and inside the normal range
- [ ] Hand near the plug: ready to disconnect at any smell, smoke or heat
